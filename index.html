Systems Quality & Requirements Lab

Research for high-quality and sustainable software systems.

SQUAREL is an interdisciplinary research lab working across software engineering, requirements engineering, human-computer interaction, sustainability, systems and software quality, and health informatics.

About

Understanding stakeholder needs and designing better software systems.

The lab focuses on understanding stakeholders’ needs and developing solutions that contribute to high-quality and sustainable software systems. Our research combines technical and human-centred perspectives to address complex challenges across diverse domains.
